Transparency & editorial standards

About WINE ELSE Journal

WINE ELSE Journal covers wine regions, trade statistics, wine markets and verified industry developments. It is part of WINE ELSE, a commercial initiative connecting French wine producers and international buyers.

Ownership and commercial disclosure

WINE ELSE Journal is published by WINE ELSE. The same brand operates commercial wine sourcing content elsewhere on else.wine. Where an article links to those pages, it identifies their commercial nature. References to wines or suppliers are not endorsements, paid placement or claims that an import deal exists.

Authorship

Articles labelled “WINE ELSE Editorial Desk” are prepared under the brand's editorial responsibility, using the sources identified in each article. They are not represented as the work of a named journalist or as exclusive first-hand reporting. Editorial personnel or business ownership should be identified more fully as verified details become publicly available.

Sources, corrections and AI assistance

We cite primary public datasets, regulators and direct statements where possible. We distinguish forecasts from final data, analysis from direct reporting and commercial opportunities from established transactions. AI tools may assist in drafting or production; facts and attribution require verification before publication. If an error is found, we aim to correct the page with a dated clarification rather than silently altering historical figures.

Photography

We use licensed or otherwise appropriately reusable imagery, with image-specific credits and license links. Archival or illustrative photographs are labelled and are not presented as pictures of current events.

Contact

Editorial corrections and business enquiries can be sent to contact@else.wine. Receipt at this address has not been independently tested in this publication workflow.
